01. About the event CodeLap
Hackathon

Ideas become code.
Code becomes impact.

CodeLap Hackathon 2026 is where caffeine, code and a few sleepless nights turn into a product that actually works.

14 student teams enter the race in Cluj-Napoca for three days, alongside mentors from the industry, to test their solutions in real conditions and take the stage in front of a jury. Two tracks to conquer, AI and DevOps, and one thing at stake: who ships first, and ships something that works.

03. Dates & schedule CodeLap
Hackathon

20 – 22 November 2026.

  1. 01

    Opening & workshops

    Friday · 20 Nov

    An AI workshop and a DevOps workshop, running in parallel. The actual challenge is announced and every team gets its questions answered.

  2. 02

    Build day

    Saturday · 21 Nov

    No workshops: a full day dedicated to building your solution, with constant support from the mentors.

  3. 03

    Final presentations

    Sunday · 22 Nov

    Each team presents its solution in about 10 minutes, followed by questions from the jury.

06. Registration

How to
take part.

Who can join

3rd and 4th year students. If you're in your third or fourth year of university and you like building software, this race is for you.

Selection

Selection is individual, based on a survey form and your CV. Teams of 3–4 selected participants are formed afterwards.
