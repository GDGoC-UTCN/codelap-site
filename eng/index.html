08. FAQ

Frequently
asked questions.

Eligibility

Who can participate?

CodeLap is open to UTCN and UBB students in their 3rd or 4th year of study, as well as those enrolled in Master's programmes.

What are the selection criteria?

Selection is made by a committee of Porsche Engineering Romania representatives, based on the information in the registration form and the participants' CVs. Motivation, experience, relevant skills and how well the team fits the chosen track are evaluated. Selection is made at team level.

Can we form a team with students from different universities or years of study?

Yes. Teams can include students from different universities and/or years of study, as long as all 4 members meet the eligibility criteria.

Schedule & logistics

When does registration start?

Registration runs from 19.10.2026 at 10:00 until 01.11.2026 at 23:59 (Romanian time).

Do I have to be on site for the whole hackathon?

Participants must be on site during the times set out in the official schedule. After 22:00, activity continues online and teams can work remotely.

What should I bring?

Bring your own laptop with a working webcam, plus any other equipment you think you'll need. We provide the workspace, a monitor for each team, internet access, meals, drinks and snacks.

Teams & participation

Can I take part if I don't have a team?

No. To register you need a complete team of 4, formed before the deadline. Each member registers individually, using the same team name and the same track.

What happens if a team member withdraws?

If a member can no longer take part, the team must inform the Organizers as soon as possible. The team can propose a replacement, who must be validated by the Organizers.

Competition

Can we start the project before the hackathon?

No. The challenge is announced at the start of the hackathon, and the competition solution must be developed exclusively during the hackathon.

Can we use existing libraries, frameworks, APIs or other resources?

Yes. Documentation, libraries, frameworks, APIs and other external resources may be used, in compliance with copyright and the applicable licences. At the request of the Organizers or the jury, the team must be able to list the resources it used.

How is the winning team decided?

Every project is evaluated against the same criteria: technical execution, presentation and demo, innovation and creativity, and relevance and impact. The team with the highest score in each track wins the 1st Prize.

Didn't find your answer? You'll find more details in the rules, or you can contact us at gdgoncampustucn@gmail.com or hackathon@porsche-engineering.ro.
